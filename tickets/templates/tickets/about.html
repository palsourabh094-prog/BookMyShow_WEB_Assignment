About BookMyShow Clone

A Django student project about finding things to do in Delhi-NCR.

Discover and book

Explore fictional films and live experiences, choose your seats and try a demo checkout. You can also rent an original short, create a gift card or submit an event inquiry.

Demo privacy

The local database stores account details, bookings, gift cards, newsletter sign-ups and inquiries. Passwords are hashed. Use sample contact details for the demonstration.

Demo terms

This independent student project uses fictional listings and simulated payments. Receipts and gift cards work within the demo. Reservations have no admission value at a cinema or venue.
